Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 Aug 2026

1. Data controller

This application ("fynanz") is self-hosted and operated for its own user by [your name], contact: [your email]. It has no operators beyond that person, and no third-party hosting is involved: the database lives on the user's own infrastructure (typically a home server behind a private network / reverse proxy).

2. Data processed

To provide the service, the app processes:

  • bank account metadata and transaction history, retrieved from your banks through Enable Banking (the open-banking aggregator) after you authorize access (PSD2 consent),
  • categories, categorization rules and notification-channel settings you create in the app,
  • no authentication data — v1 has no login; access to the app is controlled by your network boundary (a private network / reverse proxy).

3. Purposes and legal basis

Data is processed solely to provide the dashboard you asked for (aggregation, categorization, insights) at your direction. Bank data is fetched only from the accounts you whitelist in Restricted Production mode, and only while your consent sessions are active.

4. Storage and retention

All data is stored in a single local SQLite file on your own infrastructure. It is retained for as long as you use the application. Deleting the app (or its database file) erases your copies; your banks' records are governed by their own policies. Consent sessions expire per PSD2 and are re-authorized by you.

5. Recipients and subprocessors

  • Enable Banking — receives your bank credentials and data in the course of fetching it (their privacy policy at enablebanking.com applies to that transmission).
  • Your banks — the original sources of the data.
  • Notification endpoints you configure (ntfy topic, webhook URL) — receive event summaries (e.g. "new transaction needs a category") with no bank credentials.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase and receive a copy of your data. Because the app is self-hosted, these are exercised directly: everything is visible in the app, and erasure means removing the database file (backup first if needed). Contact [your email] with any question.

7. Changes

This policy changes as the app evolves; the current version is always served at this URL.
